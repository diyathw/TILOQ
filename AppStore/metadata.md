## Description

Write. Fix. Done.

TILOQ is a focused iPhone keyboard for improving selected text without opening a chatbot. Select your writing, then tap Rewrite, Grammar, or Improve to get a clean replacement powered by Apple Intelligence on your device.

FAST WRITING TOOLS

• Rewrite with a casual, professional, friendly, or shorter tone
• Fix grammar, spelling, punctuation, and capitalization
• Improve clarity, flow, and word choice
• Insert the result directly or copy it

A REAL KEYBOARD

TILOQ includes a complete QWERTY layout, number and symbol layers, suggestions, key previews, auto-capitalization, repeating delete, cursor mode, haptics, and a globe key for switching keyboards.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Writing transformations happen locally with Apple Intelligence. TILOQ has no account, analytics, advertising, or writing server. Full Access is not required.

MAKE IT YOURS

Choose a restrained animated RGB effect or a local keyboard backdrop. Appearance settings stay on your device.

OPTIONAL TEXT ENCRYPTION

Encrypt selected text with your own key text and send the encrypted result as an ordinary message. A recipient with TILOQ and the exact same key can decrypt it. TILOQ cannot recover lost keys.

Apple Intelligence availability depends on device compatibility, language, region, and system settings. Core keyboard typing and text encryption remain available without it.
