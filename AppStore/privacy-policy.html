TILOQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

TILOQ is developed by Diyath Wickramaratne, an independent app developer.

TILOQ does not collect, sell, or track personal data. Your writing is not sent to TILOQ servers.

Writing and Apple Intelligence

Rewrite, Grammar, and Improve use Apple Intelligence on your device. If the local model is unavailable, TILOQ leaves your selected text unchanged.

Keyboard data

Keyboard preferences, your optional backdrop, and encryption key text are stored locally in TILOQ's shared app container so the app and keyboard can use the same settings.

Encryption

Encryption uses AES-GCM with the key text you provide. TILOQ has no account recovery and cannot recover a lost key. Share keys separately and only with people you trust.

Clipboard

TILOQ reads or writes the clipboard only after you tap a Paste or Copy control.

Your control

You can turn off the keyboard in iOS Settings or remove locally stored TILOQ data by deleting the app.
